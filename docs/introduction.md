## Note To Markers

Currently, to engage the battle system, a user needs a minimum of 5 cards, 4 of which must not be legendary.

Due to new users having no cards, 2 accounts (which can be accessed by providing the email and password) are provided, each of which come with a selection of more than 5 cards.

The accounts can be found in the project repos `README.md`.

These accounts should allow you to access most of the features provided, including the console.
