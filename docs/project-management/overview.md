## Planned Meetings
Our planned meetings can be found [here](./meetings.md).

We decided on having three meetings weekly (with an occasional fourth
if the team feels it is necessary), mostly due to the fact
that more than that feels more like scrum and is too pressuring given
we have other responsibilities.

Three meetings seemed like the nice middle-ground for giving you time to
mention any issues or address concerns whilst still giving members 
enough time to work on their tasks.
