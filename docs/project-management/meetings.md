# Meetings

This section documents the meetings held throughout the development of the project.
Meetings are held three times per week on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays.
Each meeting is used to discuss progress, review tasks, address challenges,
and plan the next steps of development.

The project follows a Kanban-based workflow, allowing tasks to be organised, tracked,
and moved through the development process as work progresses.
